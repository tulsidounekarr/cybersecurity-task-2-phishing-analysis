\# Sample Phishing Email



\## Source



This task uses a documented phishing email example described by the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC).



Source:

https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2018/12/netflix-phishing-scam-dont-take-bait



\## Email Scenario



The phishing email impersonates Netflix and claims that the recipient's account is on hold because of a billing problem. The recipient is instructed to update their payment information through a link.



\## Reconstructed Sample



> Subject: Your Netflix account is on hold

>

> Dear Customer,

>

> We're having some trouble with your current billing information.

> Your account has been placed on hold.

>

> Please update your payment information to continue using Netflix.

>

> \\\[UPDATE PAYMENT INFORMATION]

>

> Thank you,

> Netflix Support



\## Important Note



The email shown above is a simplified reconstruction for educational analysis based on the FTC's documented phishing example. It is not presented as the original raw email or as the original email headers.



Therefore, sender addresses, URLs, IP addresses, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC results are not assumed or fabricated.



\## Purpose of Analysis



The sample will be examined for:



\- Sender spoofing

\- Suspicious links

\- URL mismatches

\- Urgent or threatening language

\- Requests for financial information

\- Social engineering techniques

\- Spelling and grammar issues

\- Email-header indicators, where original headers are available
