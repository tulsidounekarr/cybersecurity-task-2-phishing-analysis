\# Cybersecurity Internship - Task 2



\## Task: Analyze a Phishing Email Sample



\### Objective



The objective of this task is to identify phishing characteristics in a suspicious email sample and understand common phishing and social-engineering techniques.



\### Task Requirements



The task focuses on:



\- Identifying sender spoofing

\- Examining email headers

\- Identifying suspicious links or attachments

\- Detecting urgent or threatening language

\- Checking for mismatched URLs

\- Identifying spelling and grammar issues

\- Summarizing phishing characteristics



\### Sample Used



A phishing email example documented by the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) was used as the basis for the analysis.



The example involves an email impersonating Netflix and claiming that the recipient's account is on hold because of a billing problem.
